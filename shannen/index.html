My Story

From biology
to Biella

A journey across continents, disciplines, and industries — all converging on one mission: a more sustainable creative world.

Napa Valley, California

Where it began

Shannen began her academic journey as a biologist in Napa Valley, studying the degradation of the redwood forest. This work instilled a profound appreciation for nature's resilience — and a lifelong commitment to protecting it.

Paris, France

Luxury meets responsibility

For graduate school, she moved to Paris and immersed herself in the heart of luxury fashion — studying the intricate balance between high-end design and environmental responsibility. It was here she discovered that sustainability and beauty are not opposites.

Southern India

The turning point

To conclude her graduate studies, she ventured to the textile districts of Southern India, where she witnessed firsthand the devastating effects of the pollutant fashion industry on both people and the environment. India was the transformative moment where she committed her professional life to sustainable development.

Biella, Italy · Now

Building the ecosystem

Now residing in Biella — one of the world's most prestigious textile manufacturing regions — Shannen combines her scientific background in environmental science with expertise in luxury fashion and deep appreciation for cultural heritage. She leads the Kaylia Group, an ecosystem dedicated to decarbonizing and decentralizing the design industry's value chains. Her Grenadian ancestry inspired her to establish the CSDFG, empowering local women artisans by bridging traditional craftsmanship and modern sustainability.

Speaking

A voice for sustainable futures

Shannen-Kaylia speaks at the intersection of sustainability, fashion, cultural heritage, and innovation. From the United Nations to universities to global industry conferences — she brings firsthand expertise and a global perspective.
